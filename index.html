The Story

A city is watching to see which man he becomes.

A gifted, self-destructive young footballer must confront the ghost of the man who came before him — and a city that already knows how this kind of story can end.

"What does it mean to wear the Black and White — not just on the pitch, but in life?"

That's as much as we're saying for now. The rest of it is being filmed in Bulawayo this October.
